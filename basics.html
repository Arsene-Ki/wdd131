Learn the Basics

Five simple building blocks every beginner pianist needs: key names, posture, reading notes, one starter scale, and three basic chords.

1. Keyboard Layout & Note Names

Piano notes use seven repeating letters: A, B, C, D, E, F, G. To find any note fast, look at the raised black keys—they always appear in alternating groups of two and three:

  • C is always the white key right to the left of a group of two black keys.
  • F is always the white key right to the left of a group of three black keys.
  • Middle C is the C near the middle of the keyboard, right in front of where you sit.

2. Hand Position & Bench Posture

Sit toward the front half of your bench with both feet flat on the floor. Adjust the bench height so your forearms are roughly level with the keyboard, your shoulders stay relaxed, and your wrists feel flexible rather than stiff.

Keep your fingers gently curved, as if holding a small ball. Both hands use the same finger numbers: 1 = Thumb, 2 = Index, 3 = Middle, 4 = Ring, 5 = Pinky.

3. Reading Sheet Music Basics

Piano sheet music connects two five-line staves called the Grand Staff:

  • Treble Clef (top staff): Usually played by the right hand. Lines from bottom to top are E-G-B-D-F; spaces spell F-A-C-E.
  • Bass Clef (bottom staff): Usually played by the left hand. Lines from bottom to top are G-B-D-F-A; spaces are A-C-E-G.
  • Middle C: Sits on its own short line right between the treble and bass staves, acting as a bridge between your hands.
  • Basic Rhythm: Count 1 beat for a quarter note, 2 beats for a half note, and 4 beats for a whole note.

4. Starter Scale & Chords

The C major scale uses only white keys: C - D - E - F - G - A - B - C. Practice slowly one hand at a time using these standard fingerings:

Hand & Direction Notes Finger Numbers
Right Hand (Going Up) C D E F G A B C 1 - 2 - 3 - 1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5 (tuck thumb under to F)
Left Hand (Going Up) C D E F G A B C 5 - 4 - 3 - 2 - 1 - 3 - 2 - 1 (cross finger 3 over to A)

A basic triad is a three-note chord made by skipping every other white key (fingers 1-3-5 in the right hand, 5-3-1 in the left hand). Try playing these three starter chords slowly in order (C – F – G – C):

  • C Major: C – E – G
  • F Major: F – A – C
  • G Major: G – B – D
