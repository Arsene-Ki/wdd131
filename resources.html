Resources & Contact

Recommended beginner songs, method books, and practice apps—plus a form to ask a question or suggest a song.

Recommended Beginner Songs

Song Level Why It’s Good for Beginners
Mary Had a Little Lamb (Traditional) Week 1 Uses only three white keys (E, D, C) with simple stepwise motion.
Ode to Joy (Beethoven) Early Beginner Fits in a five-finger right-hand position and sounds great right away.
Minuet in G Major (Christian Petzold) Late Beginner A classic 3/4 piece from the Anna Magdalena Bach notebook for hand independence.

Helpful Books

  • Faber Adult Piano Adventures All-in-One, Book 1 — Clear step-by-step lessons, theory, and classical/popular pieces.
  • Alfred’s Basic Adult All-in-One Course, Book 1 — Introduces left-hand chords early with familiar melodies.

Useful Apps & Sites

  • musictheory.net / Tenuto — Free web drills for reading notes and intervals.
  • flowkey — Split-screen sheet music and real pianist hand videos.
  • Piano Marvel — Great for sight-reading practice and structured lessons.
