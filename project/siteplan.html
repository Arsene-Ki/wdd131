Safari Trails Tanzania

Site plan

Site Name

I chose Safari Trails Tanzania because it says exactly what the site covers: where to go and what to expect on a Tanzanian safari. The logo, a golden sun over a mountain range, will sit at the top of every page.

Domain idea: safaritrails-tz.com

Site Purpose

Safari Trails Tanzania is for people planning their first trip to Tanzania. Visitors can read about the major parks, learn when each season is best for wildlife, and send a question through a contact form.

Scenarios

  1. When should I travel to see the wildebeest migration?
  2. Which park is the best choice for a first safari?
  3. Who can I ask for help with my travel plans?
