Welcome to Keys & Keystrokes

A simple, beginner-friendly guide to finding your way around the piano keyboard, reading your first notes, and building a steady practice habit.

My Piano Journey

Playing piano is my favorite hobby away from studying and writing code. When I first sat down at a keyboard, the hardest part was not the music itself—it was knowing where to put my hands and figuring out what to practice first without getting overwhelmed.

I created this small site to share the basics that helped me most: simple ways to spot notes on the keys, an interactive keyboard right in the browser for quick practice, and a list of beginner songs and books that make learning enjoyable.

Explore the Site

  • 1. Learn the Basics — Keyboard layout, note names, hand posture, reading the staff, and your first scale and chords.
  • 2. Practice Tools — A clickable one-octave piano keyboard with sound and a practice session timer.
  • 3. Resources & Contact — Recommended beginner songs, method books, apps, and a contact form for questions or song ideas.
